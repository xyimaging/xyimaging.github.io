Xing (Alex) Yao

Computer Science . Vanderbilt University.

Nashville, Tennessee

Hello! This is Xing Yao (姚兴). I am a near-to-graduation CS Ph.D. candidate at MedICL, Vanderbilt University, advised by Prof. Ipek Oguz. Before joining Vanderbilt, I obtained my master’s degree at Duke University, advised by Prof. Roarke Horstmeyer, and my bachelor’s degree at Xi’an Jiaotong University.

I joined GE Healthcare as a summer research intern in 2024 and 2025, where I focused on developing video generation models for temporal super-resolution under large motion and noise, as well as video segmentation foundation models for high-diversity, multi-site ultrasound data.

My research interests lie at the intersection of:

  • Multimodal foundation models — vision-language, video, and medical imaging
  • Reasoning and agentic AI systems — RAG pipelines, tool-use, multi-step planning
  • Adaptive LLM systems — post-training, efficient fine-tuning, test-time compute scaling
  • Trustworthy AI — uncertainty estimation, calibration, robustness under distribution shift

I’m always open to research collaborations! If you’re interested in working together, feel free to reach out at alexyao9977 [at] gmail.com!
