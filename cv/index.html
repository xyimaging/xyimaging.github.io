Projects

  • Multi-modal geometric–semantic fusion for 3D ultrasound registration (MedICL)

    Training-free 3D ultrasound registration combining vision foundation model (DINOv3) semantics with geometric point-cloud features.

    • Coarse-to-fine matching with near real-time 2.6s/volume pair (coarse-only 0.1s/pair, ~2.5GB); ~17% accuracy improvement vs. SOTA.
    • Robust to large inter-volume motion and high noise.
  • Latent diffusion 3D outpainting for ultrasound stitching (MedICL)

    Self-supervised 3D latent diffusion outpainting to overcome sector-shaped FOV limits; nominated for MIDL 2025 best oral (top 9).

    • Inference-time 3D completion; reduced time (~40min → 59s/batch) and memory (~759MB → 21MB) vs. prior methods.
    • Mitigated FOV-related local minima; statistically significant registration gains (e.g., p<0.05, ~80% improvement reported on CV).
  • ControlNet-based synthetic data for self-supervised registration (MedICL)

    Patch-conditioned ControlNet engine for realistic ultrasound panoramas and paired transforms.

    • Controllable synthetic paired data with spatial transforms; simulated speckle, shadowing, probe motion.
    • Significant gains (>15%, p<0.05) over traditional and learning baselines without paired real-world supervision.
  • Uncertainty-guided adaptation of SAM for medical imaging (MedICL)

    Uncertainty-aware segmentation built on Segment Anything; extension toward 3D from 2D prompts.

    • +10% Dice, −52% ASSD vs. vanilla SAM on noisy images; improved robustness to imprecise box prompts.
    • False positive/negative control and calibration; 2D→3D volume segmentation from a single 2D bounding box.
  • Large field-of-view microscopy with ML imaging and classification (Duke)

    Multi-lens overlapping microscopy with a deep learning classification pipeline.

    • Expanded effective FOV at low hardware cost; ~7× faster imaging throughput and cell detection vs. single-lens setups.
